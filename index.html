Over 50 million people in India work with their voice

Teachers, singers, call-center operators, traffic personnel and public speakers. The first changes in the vocal cords are too small to hear, so most people notice only when the voice turns hoarse or disappears. Clinical tests need specialist hardware and an in-person visit. There is no simple daily check on a phone, until now.

Five steps, about ten seconds a day

  1. RecordHold one vowel sound and read one short sentence.
  2. Clean upBackground noise and silent gaps are removed. If the room is too noisy, the app asks you to try again.
  3. AnalyzeTiny changes in pitch, loudness, breathiness and clarity are measured.
  4. Compare with your normalAn AI model learns your own baseline and flags days that drift away from it.
  5. See your scoreA 0–100 Vocal Health Score, trend charts and practical tips: drink water, rest your voice, try a simple exercise.

Later: the voice as a daily health signal

VocalPulse starts with vocal strain. Once that is validated, the same recordings could help screen for other signs, guided by published research.

Breathing

Changes in breath control, linked to conditions like asthma and COPD.

Movement and speech control

Rhythm and pitch changes studied in conditions such as Parkinson’s disease.

Stress and mood

Tone, pace and energy can shift with stress and low mood.

Fatigue

Poor sleep and tiredness can show up as lower vocal energy.

Reference: Fagherazzi, G. et al. (2021). Voice for Health: The Use of Vocal Biomarkers from Research to Clinical Practice. Digital Biomarkers, 5(1), 78–88. VocalPulse is a wellness screening aid, not a medical diagnosis.
